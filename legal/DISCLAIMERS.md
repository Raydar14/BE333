## 1. Wellness, not medical advice

BE333 is a wellness-tracking and mindfulness-education product. The content in the app — breathing patterns, meditation prompts, journaling prompts, poetry prompts, day-planning templates, mantras, chanting scripts, prayer texts, gratitude prompts, stretching cues, yoga cues, how-to cards, SOS scripts, and grounding scripts — is **general educational content**. It is not medical advice, not a medical device, and not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, disorder, or condition.

If you have a health concern, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not disregard, avoid, or delay obtaining medical advice because of anything you read or do in BE333.

## 2. Not therapy — even when a BE Guide is linked

BE333 offers a "BE Guide" tier that lets a licensed clinician view a client's practice cadence and record private notes. **The BE Guide feature is not therapy.** Linking with a BE Guide inside BE333 does not by itself create a therapeutic relationship. Any professional relationship you have with a clinician outside BE333 — including informed consent, treatment planning, record-keeping, mandated reporting, and licensure — is entirely governed by that clinician's practice and applicable law, not by BE333.

If you're looking for therapy and don't have a provider, resources in your area may be reachable through:

- **US:** [findtreatment.gov](https://findtreatment.gov) or [Psychology Today's therapist directory](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists).
- **UK:** [NHS.uk mental health services](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/).
- **International:** WHO mental-health resources at [who.int](https://www.who.int/health-topics/mental-health).

## 3. Not for emergencies

**BE333 does not monitor for or intervene in emergencies.** No practice, breathing pattern, script, or feature in BE333 is a substitute for emergency care. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, is thinking about suicide, or is experiencing a mental health crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line right now:

- **United States:** call or text **988** (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) — 24/7.
- **United Kingdom:** call **116 123** (Samaritans) — 24/7, free.
- **Canada:** call or text **988** — 24/7.
- **Australia:** call **13 11 14** (Lifeline) — 24/7.
- **European Emergency:** **112** for immediate danger.
- **International directory:** [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists crisis lines by country.

The trauma-sensitive grounding content in the app's "Grounding" tab currently ends with a reminder to work with a trauma-informed clinician; the crisis-line numbers above are listed in this disclaimer document but are not yet surfaced inside that in-app screen — that is a known gap and is on the follow-up list. If you find yourself using SOS or grounding content frequently, please reach out to a clinician.

## 4. Physical practices — know your body

Stretching, yoga, breath-work, and activating breath patterns (including the 6-1-4 pattern) may not be appropriate for everyone. Do not perform them if you:

- Have a cardiovascular, respiratory, or neurological condition without first checking with your doctor.
- Are pregnant, immediately postpartum, or being treated for an eating disorder — modify or skip as your provider advises.
- Are in acute distress, having a panic attack, or feel dizzy or short of breath — the app's own copy on activating patterns says to skip them in those moments; take that seriously.

Stop any practice that hurts. Rest. Modify. When in doubt, don't.

## 5. Content is generalized

Prompts, mantras, prayer texts, and reflective questions in BE333 are drawn from broad wellness, contemplative, and religious traditions. They are provided as options, not prescriptions. Nothing in the content is endorsement of a specific faith, medical protocol, or life choice, and inclusion of a text from a tradition does not imply that tradition endorses BE333.

Users can hide all prayer content in Settings.

## 6. Third-party services

BE333 relies on third-party services (Firebase, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, RevenueCat, the Apple / Google / Stripe payment stacks, Google reCAPTCHA). Their availability and behavior are outside BE333's control. Their own privacy notices, terms, and disclaimers apply to their handling of your data.

## 7. BE Guide self-attestation

The license or registration number a BE Guide provides at signup is **self-attested and not verified by BE333**. Prospective clients considering linking with a Guide should perform their own verification through the appropriate licensing board before treating any BE Guide as a professional resource.

## 8. Session data accuracy

Optional heart-rate and heart-rate-variability samples collected during a session come from a consumer-grade Bluetooth device you connect and may contain artifacts, dropouts, or inaccuracies. Trends shown in Session History and Dashboard charts are for personal reflection, not diagnosis. Do not use BE333 to monitor a clinical condition.

## 9. Community, buddy, and shared content

- **BE Buddy:** what your buddy sees is limited to shared cadence signals for the Round. Do not send information to your buddy inside or outside BE333 that you wouldn't want them to have.
- **Linked BE Guide:** what your Guide sees is your practice cadence and any notes *the Guide* takes (which the Guide keeps). Your journal, poetry, day plans, gratitude entries, and Letter to Yourself are **not** shared with the Guide — that boundary is designed in and explicitly reiterated in the app's Privacy & Data screen.
- **Shared exports:** any plain-text export you generate via the Share feature is your responsibility once it leaves the app.
